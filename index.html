How to Play

  • Objective: Score the most points by rolling 5 dice to make combos.
  • Turns: Roll virtual dice up to 3 times. Click dice to "HOLD" them between rolls.
  • Scoring: Click on an available slot in your active column to lock in your score.
  • Upper Bonus: Score 63 or more in the upper section (Aces - Sixes) to get a +35 point bonus!
  • Joker Rule: Subsequent Yatzys earn a +100 bonus! Mark it in the Yatzy Bonus boxes, then use the wild card to fill any open slot.
